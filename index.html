Suggested study plan

How to use the playlist and these notes together.

1Watch, then read

Watch the day's video once at normal speed. Then read the page here — it is organised for retention, not for narration, so it will fill gaps the video moved past quickly.

2Type every command

Each page ends with a lab. Use a local KIND cluster; from Day 54 onward, build a real kubeadm cluster on VMs.

3Chase the Linux links

When a page says Under the hood, follow the link once. Understanding the primitive (a cgroup, a veth pair, a signal) makes the Kubernetes concept obvious and permanent.
